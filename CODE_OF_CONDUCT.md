# Code of Conduct

Relay welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and its impact; avoid personal attacks.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Help newcomers find relevant documentation and manageable tasks.
- Respect people's time, privacy, identities, and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private conversations, recordings, credentials, or personal information without permission.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts when needed to protect the community. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the conduct, and maintainers should explain decisions when doing so is safe.

For abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** control or [report abuse privately to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue. You may tag the maintainer, [@RudreshNarwal](https://github.com/RudreshNarwal), on a public thread to request moderation when no private information is involved. Security vulnerability reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy also applies when someone represents Relay in another community.
